Why these options

  • Theme — light or dark, for comfort in bright or low light.
  • Colour mode — palettes tuned for protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia, plus a high-contrast monochrome option. Status is always paired with a text label, so colour is never the only signal.
  • Text size — scales the whole interface, not just body text.
  • Font — a dyslexia-friendly typeface (OpenDyslexic). Research on whether special fonts improve reading is mixed, so treat it as a preference, and try the spacing options too, which some readers find more useful.
  • Letter, word and line spacing — more space between letters, words and lines.
  • Motion — turn off animations and transitions. "Match my device" follows your system's reduce-motion setting.
  • Windows High Contrast / forced colours — supported automatically when your system has it switched on; no setting needed.

Text to speech

Every page and every section has a Listen button. It uses your browser or device voice, so no audio is sent to us. If your browser does not support it, the button is hidden automatically.
