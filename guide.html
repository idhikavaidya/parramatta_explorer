How to use Parramatta Explorer

A quick tour. You can listen to any section with the speaker button, and change colours, contrast, text size, fonts and spacing in Settings.

For attendees

  1. 1. Explore what's on

    On the home page, search for events or filter by category. Every event is real and comes from public City of Parramatta listings.

  2. 2. Open an access plan

    Pick an event to open its Access & Inclusion Plan. You choose your needs, and the plan shows the features that matter to you.

  3. 3. Before you leave

    Check the map and each feature card. A tick means verified, a question mark means it still needs checking, and a dash means missing — the words are always shown too. Save the plan to use offline.

  4. 4. During the event

    The during section works without a signal. It shows the map and nearest facilities, and lets you report a problem on the spot.

  5. 5. After the event

    Rate how it went with one tap — good, okay or not good. It's anonymous and helps the next person.

For Council organisers

Sign in with your Council work email or an invite code. You get a readiness checklist, AI suggestions that you must approve yourself, and a risk register. Nothing is published as fact until a person approves it.

Accessibility features

  • Read aloud on every page and every section
  • Light and dark mode
  • Colourblind-friendly status palettes (protan, deuteran, tritan and monochrome)
  • Larger text sizes, a dyslexia-friendly font option, and letter, word and line spacing controls
  • Reduce-motion option, and automatic support for Windows High Contrast (forced colours)
  • Works offline: pages, plans and (once saved) the venue map
  • A plain-text list version of every map
  • Star rating and review after an event
