Browse as an attendee

We don't ask for your name, email or any health information. If you choose a need profile, it stays on your device and is never uploaded or stored on a server.

Verify you work for Council

We verify you the same way most workplaces do — no documents, no invasive checks. Use your @cityofparramatta.nsw.gov.au work email and we send a one-time link, or enter an invite code from your events team. If neither works, a Council admin can verify you manually. Demo only: no email is sent, and this check runs in your browser, so it protects nothing.

How your privacy is protected

  • Attendees: no name, no email, no health or disability information is collected or stored. Any need profile you pick is kept only in your browser.
  • Organisers: verified by a work-email link or invite code — we never ask for identity documents, and we don't store more than your verified role.
  • This is a prototype. See the About & data page for the full data approach.
