When people can trust the information, more of them take part.

Deciding whether to attend an event is hard for people with disability, older residents and families, who often can't tell if they can get in, move around or take part. That affects:

  • Wheelchair and mobility users
  • Blind and low-vision people
  • Deaf and hard-of-hearing people
  • Autistic and sensory-sensitive people
  • Older residents, carers and families
  • Culturally diverse communities

Fewer wasted trips

Checking access details first avoids wasted trips and makes attendance more likely. The same data shows Council which gaps to fix for future events.

How we'll measure it

Success is measured by verified event access details, gaps closed, and post-event feedback on whether people could take part, not just attendance. These are plans for a pilot, not results we already have.

Our vision

A Parramatta where everyone, including disabled people, older residents and families, can attend events with confidence. We propose a simple tool that gives people confidence before they travel and gives Council a live view of the barriers.

We asked the people of Parramatta.

Where the information comes from

  1. OpenStreetMap accessibility tags: candidate venue attributes (ODbL licence)
  2. Transport for NSW Open Data: accessible stops and journey context
  3. City of Parramatta event listings: name, date, venue, description
  4. Major Events and Festivals Strategy 2025–2030: engagement facts and context
  5. Council strategy documents: Disability Inclusion Action Plan, Social Sustainability Strategy, Smart City and Innovation Strategy
  6. Synthetic attendance and resource scenarios, clearly labelled as not real Council data
  7. MapLibre and OpenStreetMap tiles via OpenFreeMap for the base map

AI helps, but a person always decides

Privacy

No attendee accounts or health data. Your chosen needs stay on your device and are never sent to a server.

Tools

AI tools were used to build the website and its design.

Human check

AI only suggests access attributes or risks. A person confirms each item before publication, and each item shows its source and last-checked date.

Risks we manage

Stale or incomplete data, over-trust in AI, and exclusion when data is missing. We show where information came from and state what is unknown.

We'd ask Council to partner and advocate

Partner

Share event and venue information and provide access to spaces, so the plan is accurate.

Advocate

Encourage venues and organisers to publish accessible information.

Scope

We are not asking Council to fund or deliver a new platform, or to regulate. We propose a small shared pilot.

Fits with Council plans: Major Events and Festivals Strategy 2025–2030 (Measurement and Evaluation), Disability Inclusion Action Plan, Smart City and Innovation Strategy, Social Sustainability Strategy.
